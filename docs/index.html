How it works

Three taps to a
wall of sound.

Each device gets its own copy and they all press play at the same planned instant — then keep correcting, every second.

01

Host

Start a session on a phone or in a browser and pick a song, a video, or a browser tab to share.

02

Invite

Friends scan the QR, open the link, or type the AMP-XXXXXX code. Phones on the same WiFi find you on their own.

03

Drop the beat

Clocks are synced NTP-style, playback starts on a shared timeline, and drift correction keeps everyone within a few milliseconds.

Features

Built for real rooms.

Parties, classrooms, rooftops, road trips. Audio travels device-to-device; nothing is uploaded or stored.

NEW

Videos: picture here, sound everywhere

Pick a video on the host — from the browser or the Android app. The host shows the picture; every other device plays its sound, in sync. Phones only receive the audio, so even long films start fast.

Share a browser tab

YouTube, Spotify Web, anything in Chrome or Edge — every device plays it together, a second behind the tab.

Millisecond sync

A scheduled start plus continuous drift correction. The host sees every speaker’s offset live.

Broadcast device audio

On Android 10+, stream whatever any app is playing — or your microphone, to amplify the room.

Code, link or QR

Join from the app or any browser in seconds. No accounts, no sign-up, no tracking.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does everyone need the app?

No. Listeners can join from the Android app or straight from a browser with the code, link or QR. Phones on the same WiFi as an Android host also find it automatically.

Does it work over the internet?

Sessions hosted from the web app connect through a light internet handshake, so people can join from anywhere; the audio itself still streams device-to-device. Sessions hosted from the Android app run on the local network (same WiFi or a phone hotspot).

How do videos work?

The host shows the picture and every other device plays the sound in sync. Only the sound is sent to the phones, so they don’t download or decode a picture nobody sees.

Can I play YouTube or Spotify?

From the web app on a computer (Chrome or Edge), share the tab and every device plays it, about a second behind the tab. On Android 10+, “Broadcast device audio” streams other apps — though some apps (Spotify among them) block capture, and DRM streams are always excluded.

Why isn’t it on the Play Store?

Ampme is an independent side project, distributed directly as an APK for now. Allow “install unknown apps” for your browser or file manager when Android asks.
